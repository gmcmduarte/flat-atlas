- **Built:** `web/src/lib/map/`: `TopicMap.svelte` (the only file that imports Sigma and graphology), `MapSection.svelte` (state, toolbar, panel, URL), `TopicTree.svelte` (the keyboard interface and the list view), `EdgeLegend.svelte`, and the pure modules `model`, `visible`, `edges`, `treeNav`, `urlState`, `pageLoader`, `style`, `focusHandler` (35 new Vitest cases). Packages added with Guilherme's yes: `sigma` 3.0.3, `graphology` 0.26.0, `graphology-types` 0.24.8 (dev). Decision D-104.
- **How it works:** the drawn topics are a cut of the topic tree (`expanded` set); the positions and radii are those of `map.json`, so nothing is laid out in the browser and no collision relaxation was needed (children already sit inside the parent disc). Expanding (click, Right arrow, a search result, or zooming in on the bubble in the middle of the view) zooms the camera onto the parent's disc; an expanded topic collapses on Back, on a click on its hull, with Left or Escape, or when it is zoomed out below 60 px. Edges are not summed: D-102 stores an edge for every pair of topics, so an edge between two bubbles is a stored edge; a pair without one gets the strongest stored edge between topics inside the two bubbles (max, because the weights do not add; the legend and the tooltip say so). The map loads only the topic pages of the bubbles it draws (21 files, 384 KB, about 101 KB gzipped, on first paint); no new export file.
- **Not built:** a separate click target on a label (double click and the "Open topic page" link in the panel go to the topic page); dashed lines (Sigma has none: shared-community is a thin violet line, ghost links are thin grey); per-year sizes (P5-01); no search-result highlight beyond focusing the topic; labels of neighbouring level-0 bubbles can overlap (the layout is the pipeline's; noted below).
- **Surprises:** (1) **Sigma does not premultiply colours** but blends with `ONE, ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA`, so a translucent hull came out as a bright pale disc until the colours were premultiplied in `style.ts`. (2) With `itemSizesReference: 'positions'` (needed so that children stay inside the parent disc at every zoom) **edge thickness also scales with zoom**; thicknesses are therefore stored in pixels and converted in an `edgeReducer` on every camera change. (3) Sigma throws when its container has no width, which the list view causes (map hidden): `allowInvalidContainer`. (4) Zooming into one big bubble opened its big neighbours too; now only the bubble nearest the middle of the view opens, one at a time, and nothing opens by itself while the camera still shows all top-level bubbles (the fixture's tiny layout units made its two bubbles 200 px wide at that view). (5) At the all-topics view the roots span ±440 layout units, so a root is at most about 70 px on screen on a 640 px map; the 120 px opening threshold is never reached there on the pilot. (6) At the top level only 2 `builds-on` edges are stored between the 21 roots, but rolling up gives 16 drawn (from the roots' own pages, which list edges to subtopics); `related` 27, `shared-community` 47 (3 strongest per bubble). (7) The in-app browser pane was hidden for the whole session (the session is not open in a window), so its `requestAnimationFrame` ran at 1 Hz and could not be used; the frame rate was measured in a headed Chrome for Testing window instead (below).
